# ✈️ Vulnerability Assessment Around Shah Amanat International Airport (SAIA)

## Project Overview

This project evaluates potential vulnerable areas surrounding **Shah Amanat International Airport (SAIA), Chattogram** using GIS spatial analysis.

The study identifies educational institutions and flammable industries located within the airport's influence zone, helping assess potential safety risks and support land-use planning around critical infrastructure.

---

## Project Objectives

- Create a 5 km airport influence buffer.
- Identify educational facilities within the study area.
- Locate flammable industries near the airport.
- Visualize potential conflict zones.
- Support aviation safety and urban planning.

---

## Study Area

**Location:** Shah Amanat International Airport (SAIA), Chattogram, Bangladesh

**Buffer Distance:** 5 Kilometers

---

## Methodology

1. Obtain airport location.
2. Generate a 5 km buffer around SAIA.
3. Import education facility data from OpenStreetMap.
4. Import flammable industry locations.
5. Overlay all spatial layers.
6. Identify facilities located within the airport influence zone.
7. Produce a final vulnerability assessment map.

---

## Results

The analysis highlights the spatial relationship between:

- Airport influence zone
- Educational institutions
- Flammable industries
- High-risk surrounding areas

The final map can support decision-making for airport authorities, planners, and emergency management agencies.
